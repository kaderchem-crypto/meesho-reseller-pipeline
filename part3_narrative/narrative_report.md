## 2. Chart-Choice Justifications

1. **"Which month had the highest total revenue?" (April = INR 419,417.43, May = INR 444,594.25, June = INR 398,055.24)**
   * **Chart Type**: **Vertical Bar Chart (Column Chart)**.
   * **Justification**: A column chart is ideal for discrete categorical time comparisons (months), allowing stakeholders to accurately gauge and compare relative height differences within 10 seconds. The y-axis starts strictly at zero to maintain proportional integrity, and no 3D distortion or unnecessary legend is used since there is only a single data series.

2. **"What percentage share does Ethnic Wear represent of April's total revenue?" (INR 104,520.77 of INR 419,417.43 = 24.92%)**
   * **Chart Type**: **Donut Chart or 100% Stacked Bar Chart**.
   * **Justification**: Part-to-whole relationships are best communicated through a clean part-to-whole visual element where the message is instantly clear within 10 seconds. We avoid 3D effects and limit the slices/segments to maintain readability without cognitive overload.

3. **"How do the four regions compare on total revenue?" (North = 337,125.46, West = 333,106.33, South = 316,736.68, East = 275,098.45)**
   * **Chart Type**: **Horizontal Bar Chart**.
   * **Justification**: A horizontal bar chart facilitates clean label reading for regional names (North, South, East, West) ordered descending by value. The y-axis (or value axis) starts at zero, and a single color series is utilized without an extraneous legend, ensuring the performance gap is understood at a glance.
